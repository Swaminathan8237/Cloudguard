    Operational Safety & Non-Certification Notice

    This output is a statistical machine learning classification based on 4 physical sensor inputs (pH, TDS, turbidity, temperature), not a certified drinking-water potability declaration. Estimated model class probabilities from the trained Decision Tree are empirical leaf node sample proportions and do not guarantee real-world water safety. Physical sensors cannot detect microbiological pathogens (e.g., E. coli, Salmonella, noroviruses), toxic heavy metals (e.g., arsenic, lead, mercury), or hazardous organic chemicals. Standardized laboratory testing under statutory drinking-water standards (such as WHO guidelines or BIS IS 10500) remains strictly necessary to establish actual potability. Physical equipment actuation (valves, pumps) is strictly disabled.
